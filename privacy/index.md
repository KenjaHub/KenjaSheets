# Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026 · Applies to the Kenja Sheets macOS app

## In short

Kenja Sheets does not collect, transmit, or share your personal data. We operate no servers, run no analytics, and have no accounts. Your spreadsheets and settings live on your Mac, and AI requests go directly from the app on your Mac to the AI provider you configure.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your documents.** Every spreadsheet, folder, project, and version snapshot is stored locally on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded to us — there is nothing to upload to.
- **App settings.** Preferences, window state, and model configuration are stored locally.
- **Original files.** When you import an Excel or CSV file, the original file is never modified or moved.

## AI requests

Kenja Sheets lets you bring your own model: you connect any OpenAI-compatible endpoint (for example DeepSeek, Qwen, or a model running on your own machine). When you use AI features:

- The app sends your message, the relevant sheet content, and document names **directly from your Mac to the endpoint you configured**. We never see this traffic — it does not pass through any Kenja service.
- How that provider handles your data is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. If you prefer, connect a local model and your data never leaves the machine.
- Without a model configured, the app is simply a spreadsheet and works fully offline.

## API keys

API keys for your AI providers are stored only in the macOS Keychain on your Mac. They are never included in documents, exports, backups of app data, or anything sent to us.

## App Store

Kenja Sheets is distributed through the Mac App Store. Any purchase, download, or update information is processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy; we receive no such information.

## Analytics and tracking

There are none. The app contains no analytics SDKs, no trackers, no ads, and makes no requests except to the AI endpoint you configure.

## Children

Kenja Sheets is a productivity tool, not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
